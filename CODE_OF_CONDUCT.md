# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in CellQuorum, whether as a user, a lab member or a contributor, to be a
respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a good community:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning, including people new to programming or new
  to single-cell analysis.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the project and the
  science rather than on who is right.
- Saying plainly when a result is uncertain, and crediting other people's work.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [SecondBook5](https://github.com/SecondBook5), using
the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use the public issue tracker. Reports
are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove or edit comments,
contributions and issues, and may ban people who break these standards.
